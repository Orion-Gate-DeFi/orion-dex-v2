What's a swap?

You're trading one token for another at the best available rate right now. No order book, no counterparty.

What's the fee?

The network charges gas to process your transaction. It's paid in the native token of the source chain, not a platform fee.

Why "verified"?

We check the destination token against known-safe lists and on-chain scam signals before you commit.

Why approve first?

Tokens require a one-time permission so the exchange contract can move them. We only request what this swap needs.
